## Leafy Grass

- Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/leafy_grass
- Author: Charlotte Baglioni
- Files used: `leafy_grass_diff_2k.jpg`, `leafy_grass_nor_gl_2k.png`, `leafy_grass_rough_2k.jpg`, `leafy_grass_ao_2k.jpg`
- Purpose: primary grass surface and subtle ground-color variation

## Dirt

- Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/dirt
- Author: Charlotte Baglioni
- Files used: `dirt_diff_2k.jpg`, `dirt_nor_gl_2k.png`, `dirt_rough_2k.jpg`, `dirt_ao_2k.jpg`
- Purpose: exposed soil and the transition between grass and rock

## Rock 01

- Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_01
- Author: Rob Tuytel
- Files used: `rock_01_diff_2k.jpg`, `rock_01_nor_gl_2k.png`, `rock_01_rough_2k.jpg`, `rock_01_ao_2k.jpg`
- Purpose: steep slopes and exposed castle-hill rock

All downloaded maps are the 2K variants. OpenGL normal maps are used because Unity imports them without a green-channel inversion.
